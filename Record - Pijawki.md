#### PIJAWKI, THE BLOOD DEITIES

The ascended beings, known as Pijawki(Pijakwa if singular), are former mortals who have transcended into divine entities. Their nature and Domain are influenced by their path to ascension and their Paragon, and their power can be inferred by their size and amount of worship they receive. While they are powerful beings on their own, worship and reverence further fuel their power, making a religious following a must of any Pijawka. Worshiping a Pijawka grants plenty of protection to its followers as well, creating a symbiotic relationship between deity and mortal. The way reverence is shown to one's deity can differ vastly from cult to cult, but prayers and sacrificial rites are the most common.

#### BASTIONS

Pijawki exist in a corporal state known as Bastions, a physical manifestation of their bodies. This is a nature inherited from their mother God, Prawia, who also has a corporal body though on a much larger scale. Bastions are living structures that can range that serve as a place where a god stores its heart, as temples of worship and as safe havens for mortals seeking refuge from the rabid wilderness.  The presence of a Bastion strengthens the faithful, empowering those who seek its guidance and follow its creed. Where a Bastion is located is quite predictable, as a Bastion will alway lay on a major artery of Prawia as despite being such powerful beings, without a steady supply of The blood of Prawia they will begin to lose power and even potentially perish. This is why, despite being divine and ascended beings, they are referred to as parasitic and blood sucking leeches(Pijawki).

While Bastions are where a Pijawkas influence is at its strongest, it is also a point of weakness for them as this is where their hearts are stored. The heart of a deity it's quite literally a heart, as it is a fleshy beating mass that pumps its divine blood around its body. A heart is the core of a god; if it is destroyed the god will also perish, thus its protection becomes a top priority. As such Pijawki will help their followers to become more powerful in exchange for their aid in defending their Bastions, and essentially make their bodies into living fortresses filled with traps, beasts and other deterrences.

These hearts are very desirable as if a Pijawka consumes another’s heart, it will grow vastly in power. Deities will go to war with one another for that very reason, in order to dominate and consume their opponent. Though, consuming a Pijawkas heart is not only for the divine; If a mortal consumes their heart, they will immensely come into godhood, inheriting its domain. That is of course if they manage to keep their sanity that comes with such a rapid rise in power and divine status.

#### PARAGONS

Pijawki can be categorized into 4 paragons:

- Creation - The architects of life, industry and relentless expansion.
- Destruction - The harbingers of war, calamity and obliteration.
- Indulgence - The patron of excess, pleasure and unbridled hedonism
- Discipline - The embodiment of order, control and unwavering law.

These paragons exist on opposing extremes that cannot coexist together in a god, though a god can be of two paragons if they are neighboring. For example, deities can embody discipline alone or discipline and destruction, however they cannot embody discipline and indulgence at the same time. Paragons also dictate the likelihood of deities getting along, as you see more Pijawki allying and peacefully coexisting with each other if they are of the same paragon instead of if they were opposing.

These paragons outline how a deity is worshiped and more or less what their ideas and personality are. These paragons are often reelected onto the shape of a Bastion: take for example two deities of opposing paragons, Mokosh(indulgence) and Prone (discipline). The latters Bastion is a humongous temple full of studied priests, with its center being a courtyard filled with constant debate, judgment and enforcement of holy laws. The former's Bastion is on the other hand is a sprawling garden hidden away within the rabid wilderness, filled with fruits that can put even hatching deities into ecstasy with one bite, with its center being a throne of constantly writhing bodies where her avatar sits and watches in pleasure.

Prawia itself does not fit into any of these paragons directly, it is unknown if this is because it encapsulates all Paragons, or none of them.

#### DOMINIONS

A Pijawki’s Dominion is what that deity holds authority over and can control. A deity can hold multiple Dominions provided it is powerful enough or if it swallows another Deities heart(provided they are not of opposite paragons). A Pijawki’s Dominion is reflected in how it is worshipped.

#### VEINS

To mitigate the corrupting effects of Prawia’s blood, ancient sages devised a system to guide ascension: the Veins of Prawia. By following a god’s teachings and treading the path of a deity before them, mortals can grow in power with a reduced risk of madness. In return, gods gain strength through the devotion of their followers. Worship and sacrifice are essential in this exchange, though the line between devotion and servitude is often blurred.

#### WORSHIP

Worship is an interesting topic, as its meaning shifts depending on whose eyes you view it through. From the Lowly perspective, it is a means of protection and a way to find purpose in something greater than themselves. From the Nobles' perspective, while many share the same views as the Lowly, their understanding is tinged with a hint of cynicism. They recognize that worship is not a one-sided transaction—there is always a price to pay for their devotion to a Pijawka. However, they also keenly understand its benefits.

Deities, meanwhile, see worship as a currency—a means of amassing power. The reverence they receive and their influence go hand in hand. As such, deities understand that more influence is always better. A subtle balance of worship exists in each city, with even Bastions usually hosting churches of other gods. A god can benefit from a worshipper even if that worshipper follows multiple gods, so deities of similar Paragons often encourage the worship of their fellow Pijawki within their borders, expecting reciprocity in return.

One can follow multiple Veins, causing their abilities to converge, though doing so increases the risk of going rabid. By treading multiple paths, one essentially begins paving a new path, becoming half-Veinless. However, this risk is mitigated if the gods one follows share highly similar or complementary Veins. One can perform rituals and sacrificial rites for multiple gods at once. However, it must be noted that worshipping a god and following their Vein are two different things. While worshipping multiple gods is accepted, following multiple Veins is much more discouraged unless the gods are of a similar Paragon.

#### THE VEINLESS

The Veinless are those who drink Prawia’s blood without following a Vein. These individuals possess an enormous resistance to madness or have found alternative methods to stave off its effects, such as artifacts. They are often powerful, ambitious, and clear-minded individuals who forge their own paths. The Veinless are also the most likely to ascend and serve as the protectors of the handful of godless cities scattered throughout the world.

What makes the Veinless unique is that their powers are entirely their own, rather than being mere imitations of a god’s abilities. This makes them difficult to predict, as their strength and abilities do not follow a predefined pattern.

#### DIVINE ASCENSION

A Pikawki’s ascension follows an arranged pattern. It all begins with a Noble becoming powerful enough for them to begin their divine metamorphosis by cacconing themselves into a structure known as an Obelisk. It is unknown when exactly a Noble is ready for this metamorphosis, but as a general rule, the more powerful an entity is, the more likely they are to ascend. Though, there are some exceptions to that with sometimes even the Lowly ascending by forming cults around themselves and becoming able to form into an Obelisk just from the reverence they received from their followers. As an Obelisk is a bastion on a smaller scale, it must be placed on a vein of Prawia and the individual must shed their fleshy bodies in order to become a divine structure instead. If an Obelisk is not formed in an area where it can feed of Prawias blood efficiently, it will quickly perish and the ascension will fail. The ascension itself can take a very long time to complete and it often ends in failure even for the most prepared. How one ascends is not something known to the majority of the populace and due to its risks, very little of those who know of it are willing to attempt it. Many mortals who seek godhood as such are more likely to try to slay a young Pijawka and consume their heart rather than to bear the trials of ascension by themselves.

After becoming an obelisk and successfully ascending, the hatchling Pijawka must then feast as much as it can in order to increase its power as it climbs the divine hierarchy.
