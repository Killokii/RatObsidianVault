Backstory

Yudir wasn’t just a smuggler—he was in the business of moving magic for unsavory individuals. Wands, scrolls, enchanted weapons—if it had power, he knew someone willing to pay for it. Some called him a fence, others a black-market dealer, but he liked to think of himself as a businessman.

That was until a deal went wrong after a betrayal from one of his colleagues. He got caught with an illegal artifact—something important enough to land him in Revel’s End. He might’ve rotted there, but fate had other plans. With the dawn on a new lich on these lands, death struck most people in ten town. Luckily he survived this massacre, and even luckier, he managed to slip away from the prison in all the chaos. 

Now free, he was going to try to leave tentowns and get back to his old territory, but he caught wind of an interesting rumor of an ancient city filled with magic hidden in the glacier. On top of that, there was a party that was searching for it.
