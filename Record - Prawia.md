Those who succumb to the corrupting influence of Prawia’s blood become Rabid—frenzied, monstrous beings driven by an insatiable blood hunger. The Rabid are unpredictable and violent, ranging from mindless beasts to former Nobles whose divine potential has twisted them beyond recognition.

Prawia itself is not merely an alternate version of earth, but a vast, living entity that is both a planet and a god. Prawia’s body flows with holy blood, a powerful and sacred substance that the inhabitants of its surface have learned to extract and harness. This divine essence possesses immense mystical properties, serving as a catalyst for magic, a medium for crafting enchanted artifacts, and even a source of physical empowerment when consumed. However, the consumption of Prawia’s blood is a double-edged sword: while it can grant extraordinary strength, it also carries the risk of erosion, twisting the mind and body into something monstrous.

Over time, those who have continuously ingested Prawia’s blood undergo a transformation—ascending to godhood. Their mortal forms gradually dissolve, giving way to sprawling Bastions—immense cities, grand temples, or enigmatic fortresses that become living embodiments of their being and their divinity. These Bastions, along with the towns and cities that worship them, serve as sanctuaries against the dangers that lurk beyond their borders.
