## Elevator Pitch

A computer arrives mysteriously at your doorstep. On it, an online forum awaits — filled with fellow survivors fostering connection, sharing stories, and trying to survive together.

Explore this newfound community and piece together what happened to the world through their fragmented conversations, personal sites, and shared memories.

Inspired by the sense of community — and underlying dread — experienced during the pandemic lockdowns, _[Forum Name]_ highlights how the internet can be a beacon of hope and humanity in dark times.

---

## Gameplay Overview

The core gameplay revolves around exploring a post-apocalyptic internet.

Through casual browsing of forum posts, personal websites, and user-created content, players will uncover the story of post-apocalypse Australia, the nature of the disaster, and how these "netizens" adapted to life online.

Rather than being directed by strict objectives, the game encourages freeform discovery, evoking the feeling of a 3 AM internet rabbit hole — where players follow what genuinely interests them.

The game seeks to showcase the internet in a positive light, emphasizing themes of resilience, community, and connection.

---

## Users

A small group of **prominent forum users** will each have unique personalities, posting styles, and personal websites.  
Each user will feel distinct, helping the player become invested in the community and uncover different perspectives on the world.

## Setting: The World

The game is set in **Australia**, roughly **10 years after the apocalypse**.

The apocalypse involved blood raining from the sky as eldritch beings descended, cutting off all electricity and communication overnight. Humanity was pushed to the brink of extinction but managed to survive in small, isolated communities or lone bunkers.

Years later, a tech genius — from his secure base — built a rudimentary server and re-established a form of internet. He traveled across regions, distributing handmade computers and teaching people how to build and repair their own machines.

This new, fragile network forms the backbone of the world the player explores.

## Art Style

The visual aesthetic is **gritty**, **rough**, and **cobbled-together** — as if the new internet was rebuilt by survivors in basements.  
A mix of **neo-city decay** and **personal, intimate spaces**, influenced by:

- _Pony Island_
    
- _That Hell Roguelike_
    
- _Signalis_
    
- _Inscryption_
