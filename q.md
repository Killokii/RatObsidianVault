The core gameplay of Apocosite revolves around exploring a post-apocalyptic internet. Through casual browsing of forum posts, personal websites, and user-created content//too clinicals, players will 

uncover happenings around the current apocalypse, the nature of the disaster and how netizens adapted to their new lives.

This game is primarily narrative-driven, with writing and visuals conveying the story. Rather than following a single overarching plot, Apocosite focuses on small character interactions and Character self expressions that reveal more about the world, the people in it, and the everyday situations they face. As such, the game does not have any true objectives and instead encourages freeform exploration — evoking the feeling of a 3 AM internet rabbit hole, where players whimsically follow whatever piques their curiosity.

This project is deeply inspired by the sense of community — and the underlying dread — I experienced during the pandemic lockdowns. It reflects how the internet became a refuge for many: a space where people expressed themselves across different sites, formed connections, and simply hung out together online despite feeling like the world was ending.

The game will emulate an actual browser interface, with functionality focused around the forum and related content.

Core gameplay features:

- Exploring forum posts, user profiles, and personal websites.
    
- Piecing together the world’s history through ambient storytelling.
