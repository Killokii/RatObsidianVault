To perform hematurgy, one must be in possession of two aspects: a Thorn and an organ called Prawia's Rena. Without these two, creating magical phenomena is nigh impossible—at least without the divine intervention of a Pijawka.

##### Now, what are Prawia's Renas?

Let us first discuss the more important aspect of hematurgy: Prawia's Rena, or Rena for short, is an organ located below the heart which is able to imbue one's blood with magical properties, create more blood, as well as filter Prawia's blood into one's own. Without it, one's blood will not have enough power to be used for any acts of hematurgy—and worse, one will not be able to ingest the blood of Prawia without it. The issue is, though, that if one is not born a noble, they will not have a Rena in their body. This is why there is such a big gap in power between the Noble and the Lowly: a noble, unlike the Lowly, can use hematurgy straight out of the womb. It must be noted that, like with all things, there are exceptions to this. There are rare cases of a Lowly being born with a Rena, and vice versa. (To give an example, my crimson-armored friend is one of the few lowly I know born with a Rena.)

For the Lowly, the issue of a Rena has to be overcome with wit rather than force. Through a quite gruesome—and honestly quite dangerous—surgery, a Lowly can get a synthetic Rena embedded into their body. These synthetic Renas are, sadly, not a perfect substitute for an organic one, and as such require regular upkeep and outpoints somewhere on one's body to let out the huge amounts of heat they create. From personal experience, it is like having a little sun in your chest. Heat flashes and fevers come commonly with it inside one's body, and sometimes, when overused, it gets to the point that your whole body becomes red and let's off steam like an out-of-control locomotive. I've heard of cases of people being cooked alive from the inside out from faulty Renas and/or overuse of them. There have even been explosions from the inside out documented—quite nasty stuff. Though, these are the dangers the less fortunate have to endure to match natural-born capabilities.

This is why, although the Lowly make up the vast majority of the population, the amount of Lowly who can perform acts of hematurgy is actually quite small. Well, this and the fact that this method—on top of the dangers and cost of upkeep—still requires an egregious cost to be done or/and great connections to be acquired.

It must be noted that Renas (both synthetic and organic) can be consumed for an increase in power. It is even said that if a Lowly eats enough of these organs, they can grow their own.


#### The Tools of hematurgy: Thorns and Verin
Thorns are essentially both magical armaments and catalysts for magic. They are a warrior’s best friend as well as a priest’s core companion. A Thorn’s capability for customization is quite wide, and one perfectly tailored to one's lifestyle can often be found without trouble (provided one has the coin—though most who get over the first barrier of hematurgy, the Rena, usually do not care about such amounts of money anymore).

Thorns, unlike the more standard weapons used by those who cannot perform hematurgy, are made out of a unique metal derived from Prawia’s blood.

What differentiates these tools from their non-magical counterparts is the fact that they are quite gluttonous: they must drink blood. This fact is not known to the general public, but all Thorns are, in fact, alive. The metal they are made of is one giant, single-celled, and ever-growing organism called Verin.

As Verin is an organism made out of Prawia’s blood and has been dug out from deep within her core, it is debated in scholarly circles whether Verin is, in fact, a piece of Prawia herself—though I personally believe that it is not. Verin, quite similar to an amoeba, is an organism that can be infinitely sliced and divided, yet still conjoin into itself and thus be reused. There were many quite funny cases at the beginning of Thorn usage where they were not properly tempered—so when weapons clashed into one another, they sometimes conjoined and melded together mid-battle.

Verin, unlike actual metal, must be fed to stay alive and can die if neglected. Once starved, Verin loses many of its magical properties and acts more like a standard metal. While Dead Verin can no longer be used for Thorns, it is commonly repurposed for everyday magical devices and weapons for the Lowly. The most common use for Dead Verin is in our universal currency, _Krev_, with our coins being made out of it.

The Verin and its user form a symbiotic relationship: the user channels the Verin for casting and power, while the Verin uses the user as sustenance.

There are two different bodies of Verin cultures, commonly called Warm and Cold Verin. These two cultures cannot be melded into one another and exhibit some different properties. These two types were mined out during the dig into Prawia’s core before she hardened her earth, so it is unknown if more types of Verin might exist.

**Ogierin** (_Warm Verin_) is always warm to the touch—just shy of being scalding. Ogierin Thorns specialize in casting at longer ranges, and the skills that can be performed through them are quite varied. Unlike their cold counterparts, they can be used to cast standardized hematurgic formulas. They also bond with the user by strengthening their Rena, increasing blood production and quality. Ogierin is flexible to the user’s needs; however, it leaves the user’s body vulnerable. This is the type of Verin favored by mages and priests, and the one I personally use myself.

**Zimarin** (_Cold Verin_) is always cold as ice to the touch, no matter the weather. Zimarin is better suited to weapons used in close combat and cannot perform hematurgy at distances farther than mid-range. This Verin also struggles with casting a wide variety of skills and is instead better suited for performing a few with greater finesse. Zimarin bonds with its user in a way that strengthens the body. It is most often used for melee weapons and armor, with warriors being its main users.


##### more on synthetic Renas

There is only one tool in existence that has managed to marry these two types of Verin together, and that is the synthetic Rena. In fact, the synthetic Rena uses three types of Verin, as dead Verin is used as a neutral skeleton for its body.

Of course, the two living types of Verin in the Rena are not compatible at all. Rather than being  melded into one another, they are carefully balanced in order to try to keep each other’s negatives in check. Because Warm Verin has more functions similar to the Rena than its cold variant, it usually takes up a larger ratio of the synthetic Rena. It also helps that Cold Verin keeps the Warm Verin better in check than the inverse—for some unexplained reason. 


#### Needles
Accessories, rings, amulets, piercings, or embedded shards made out of Verin. They are used to regular used to channel, regulate, or amplify blood magic. Like Thorns, they must be fed blood to function.  they are made quite differently than thorns thus don't have their casting capabilities.
