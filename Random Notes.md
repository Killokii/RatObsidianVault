Aesthetic overview 
The **visual aesthetic** will be gritty, a bit amateurish, and cobbled-together — reflecting that this was made by people rediscovering the internet for the first time in years. It will be highly inspired by the early 2000s internet with deliberately clunky layouts, aggressive colors and a lot of experimental expression.

While the **Apocosite forum** will have a distinct and uniform style, people's own **Apocosites** will be highly personalized: no Apocosite will be like the other. The only thing that will string the visuals into something coherent will be the top browser bar and light pixelated distortion over the top of everything.  These aspects are reflected in the current pitch deck! 

The closest reference for the aesthetic Apocosite will be aiming for is the official _Inscryption_ website, which features a small navigable desktop!

---

The Apocosite forum will be accessible via a computer set in a 3s apace, with mouse and keyboard visible. This 3d space will be created in a PSX style and will contain subtle bits of visual story telling about the current world. Eg: A note pad containing a reminder to go out scavenging for food.

---

Apocosites is heavily inspired by Neocities websites and will borrow much of the visual language commonly used by their creators—tiled backgrounds, blinkies, buttons, emoticons, chaotic GIFs, and more. However, Apocosites will feature its own in-house collection of these elements, all reimagined and contextualized within a post-apocalyptic internet setting.


As the game will be very much inspired by nrocity websites, I will be styling the Apocosites in a way that will replicate them. I will be making such things like a reusable library of emoticons/icons, background tiles, blinkies and buttons in house that will convery the internet culture and  in this post pocolyptic internet
