Certain beings possess a higher concentration of Prawia’s blood, making them stronger and more revered. These creatures, known as Nobles, are seen as holy and are often treated with respect. Their divine essence causes them to shed human characteristics, resulting in inhuman features such as elongated stature, unnatural pigmentation, and, most notably, non-human pupils. Some even discard their human forms entirely, taking on the shapes of beasts or incomprehensible entities.

A Noble’s transformation is further influenced by their chosen god. Those who dedicate themselves to a specific deity undergo physical mutations reflecting their patron. For example, the Kin of Mokosk, develop avian features, growing feathers, talons, and massive wings. These hereditary transformations have led to the concept of racial lineages (kin).

Nobility is also an inheritable trait, passed down through bloodlines. The concentration of Prawia’s blood within a Noble can be transferred to their offspring, reinforcing the strength of certain lineages over generations. However, Nobles can only reproduce with lowly beings or individuals who share a similar bodily makeup and worship. This restriction further strengthens the distinction between different kin and prevents uncontrolled mutations or excessive corruption.

Nobles also possess vastly extended lifespans, far surpassing those of the lowly. However, while lowly beings fear the inevitability of death from old age, Nobles live with a different terror: erosion. The longer a Noble exists, the greater their risk of succumbing to madness, as prolonged exposure to Prawia’s blood steadily erodes their sanity. This ever-present threat ensures that even the mightiest of Noble beings do not remain untouched by the passage of time. As such, the more [Noble] a being is, the more vulnerable they are to erosion—a state of madness caused by prolonged exposure to Prawia’s blood. Nobles are prone to erratic, volatile behavior, making them as revered as they are feared.
